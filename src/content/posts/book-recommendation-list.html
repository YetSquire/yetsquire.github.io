Book Recommendation List

05/06/2026

This is a list of writings I’ve read that I would recommend to just about anyone.    Including you 🫵

Last updated 05/06/2026

Nonfiction

  • Liberty’s Exiles
  • Creativity Inc.– any would-be creative or organizer should read this
  • Breakneck
  • It Doesn't Take a Hero: The Autobiography of General H. Norman Schwarzkopf
  • Geek Heresy – reminded me that hard problems (generally) don’t have easy solutions

Fiction

  • Stormlight Archive– a self-help book stretched into a vast fantasy series
  • The Summer War
  • Murderbot Chronicles
  • Scholomance– read it as a teenager, and helped me understand evil as something people are driven to, instead of born with.
  • Foundation
  • Jade City

Blogs & Misc.

  • JOINT STRIKE FIGHTER AIR VEHICLE C++ CODING STANDARDS (Lockheed Martin Technical Doc) – An excellent piece of technical writing I wish more engineers read and applied
  • America Against America (Book)
  • Those Who Walked Away from Omelas (Short Story)
  • The Whispering Earring (Short Story) – has many allegories, but I read it as the need to explore
  • The Man in the Arena (Speech)
  • https://asteriskmag.com/issues/05/why-you-ve-never-been-in-a-plane-crash (Blog) – almost every area of life can be improved with a blameless postmortem
  • Newton’s Flaming Laser Sword (Blog)
